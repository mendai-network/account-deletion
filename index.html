Account privacy

Delete your Mendai account

Request permanent deletion of your Mendai account and account-linked data—even if you no longer have access to the Mendai app.

1

Submit your request

Use the email linked to your Mendai account. A username is helpful but optional.

2

Ownership may be verified

Mendai support may contact you before processing the request to protect your account.

3

Account data is removed

Account-linked user data is deleted. Limited security, billing, audit, or legally required records may be retained.

Request account deletion

If you still have Mendai installed, the fastest option is Settings → Account actions → Delete account.

!
Permanent action. Once your identity is verified and the request is processed, your Mendai account cannot be restored. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription.

This form never asks for your password.
